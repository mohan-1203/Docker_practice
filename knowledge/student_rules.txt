Student Performance Rules

Rule 1:
Students with attendance above 75% and study hours above 4 hours per day
are generally considered likely to pass.

Rule 2:
Students with attendance below 60% are considered high risk.

Rule 3:
Students with attendance above 80% and study hours above 6 hours per day
are considered strong performers.

Rule 4:
Students with attendance between 60% and 75% require improvement
in either attendance or study hours.

Rule 5:
Students studying less than 2 hours per day are considered at risk
regardless of attendance.
